In her past life, Lemira was the perfect little sister—obedient to her brothers in every way. But her devotion was met with betrayal. Her brothers took her resources, trampled on her dignity, and lavished it all on their precious “adopted sister,” Evelina. Lemira, their real sister, was cast out and left to die alone.
Reborn with a new lease on life, Lemira swears by a single mantra: no more helping, no forgiveness, no reconciliation. They can stick together—I’ll rise on my own.
But without Lemira, her brothers’ once-thriving lives crumble. As they finally realize her worth, they fall to their knees, begging for her forgiveness. “Lemmy, please come back! We’re family—we share the same blood!”
Lemira’s icy smirk says it all: “Should’ve thought of that before. Sorry, but forgiveness isn’t on my agenda.”
This time, Lemira is unstoppable. Will her brothers ever win her back? Or will she show the world just how powerful one woman can be?
